**First principle:** Grow adoption through demonstrable clarity and trust.

## Positioning

Proposed promise: Set document access where you work, and understand the rule before you save. Pair the claim with upload/property task evidence and live retrieval enforcement tests.

## Claims discipline

Do not claim best on the market, automatic legal compliance, DRM/encryption or anonymous sharing. Labels are enforced inside EdgeQuake; downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Competitive capabilities are benchmark inputs, not strawman weaknesses.

## Activation

Measure correct first labeled upload, successful in-context edit and accurate audience comprehension. Track support confusion around Public, all-required tags and unverified. More security-control clicks alone are not adoption.

## Onboarding

Explain the two dimensions with one concrete example: Confidential plus HR and Atlas needs the clearance level and both tags. Provide role-appropriate guidance and a link to authorized Settings; do not block basic upload with enterprise administration.

## Experiment guardrails

Experiment with copy/hierarchy/help timing only. Never change enforcement, defaults to a weaker reviewed label, or hide downgrade warnings to improve completion. Analytics contains no filenames, tags, members or reason text.

## Edge and test focus

EC-180-04/13/22/23/33; T-180-04/13/22/23/33 plus tasks A–G in the research protocol. Public superiority claims require licensed/configured like-for-like comparisons and larger-sample evidence.

## Implementation ownership

Lead WP-08 task study and honest client migration/onboarding copy; Product and Security approve claims. Publish only measured task-specific advantages, with configuration/date and limitations.

The lens is a review perspective applied within this pack, not a claim of a separate independent expert review. Resolve conflicting recommendations against [the policy laws](../01-first-principles.md), then record a concrete requirement/test change.
