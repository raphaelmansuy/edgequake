# EdgeQuake Datalayer Improvements E2E Test Document

Sarah Chen is the Chief Technology Officer at Acme Robotics, a company based in
San Francisco. She previously worked at Globex Corporation alongside Dr. Raj
Patel, who now leads the AI research division at Acme Robotics.

Acme Robotics partnered with Initech in 2024 to build an autonomous logistics
platform called PathFinder. The PathFinder project is managed by Sarah Chen and
uses a knowledge graph engine developed by Raj Patel.

Globex Corporation acquired Initech in early 2025, making Maria Lopez — the CEO
of Globex — an indirect stakeholder in the PathFinder platform. Maria Lopez and
Sarah Chen co-authored a paper on graph-based retrieval at the SIGIR conference.

The PathFinder platform runs on PostgreSQL with the Apache AGE extension and the
pgvector extension for similarity search. Raj Patel chose pgvector because of its
HNSW index support, which Sarah Chen validated during the pilot in San Francisco.
