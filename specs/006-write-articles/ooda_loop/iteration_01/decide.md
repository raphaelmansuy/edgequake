## 🎯 Prioritized Actions for This Iteration

### Decision 1: Create Article 001 - Why Classic RAG Fails

**Rationale**: This is the "hook" article that creates problem awareness. It must:

- Start with WHY (pain point)
- Be highly shareable (stops the scroll)
- Lead readers to want the solution

### Decision 3: Content Blueprint for Article 001

#### Medium Article Structure

1. **Title**: "Why Classic RAG Doesn't Work (And What To Do About It)"
2. **Subtitle**: "The hidden flaw in vector search that's killing your AI's accuracy"
3. **Hook**: The "lost relationship" problem
4. **Body**:
   - Problem 1: Chunks are isolated (no relationships)
   - Problem 2: No global understanding
   - Problem 3: No multi-hop reasoning
   - Solution teaser: Knowledge graphs
   - Diagram: Traditional RAG vs Graph-RAG
5. **CTA**: "In the next article, we'll explore how EdgeQuake solves this..."

#### LinkedIn Post Structure

1. **Hook (Line 1)**: "Your RAG system is lying to you."
2. **Problem**: 3 bullet points on RAG failures
3. **Insight**: The relationship problem
4. **Diagram**: ASCII visualization
5. **CTA**: "Full deep-dive in comments 👇"

#### X.com Thread Structure

1. **Tweet 1**: Bold claim hook
2. **Tweet 2-4**: The 3 problems
3. **Tweet 5-7**: Why this happens
4. **Tweet 8-10**: Visual diagrams
5. **Tweet 11-12**: Solution teaser
6. **Tweet 13**: Summary
7. **Tweet 14**: CTA
