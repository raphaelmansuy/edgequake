### Root Cause Discovery

Inspected generated vs gold tables:

```
Generated: | Term Extraction | A1.2 - Scholarly | 0.4578 | 4 |
Gold:      | Term Extraction | A1.2 Scholarly | 0.4578 | 4 |
```

**Problem:** Generated cells contain extra `-` characters!

**Hypothesis:** The lattice extraction is including graphical line elements (`---` separator lines) as text content within cells.
