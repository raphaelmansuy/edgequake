# EdgeQuake Test Document

## Overview
EdgeQuake is a knowledge graph RAG platform that enables intelligent document processing and retrieval.

## Key Entities

### Sarah Chen
Sarah Chen is the lead developer at TechCorp who designed the EdgeQuake architecture. She graduated from MIT in 2015 and has 10 years of experience in distributed systems.

### TechCorp
TechCorp is a technology company founded in 2010, headquartered in San Francisco. The company specializes in AI and machine learning solutions.

### EdgeQuake Project
The EdgeQuake project was started in 2024 by Sarah Chen at TechCorp. It aims to revolutionize document understanding through knowledge graphs.

## Technical Details
EdgeQuake uses Rust for the backend, React for the frontend, and PostgreSQL with pgvector for vector storage.
